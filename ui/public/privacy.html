Privacy Policy

Open EMS (Vyriy EMS) · Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy describes how the Open EMS mobile app and website (https://220-km.com:9220/) handle information. Operator: 220 km (220-km.com). Contact: km220.com@gmail.com.

What the app is

Open EMS is an energy-management dashboard for solar, battery (BESS), and EV charging sites. The iOS app is a native shell that loads the Open EMS web dashboard.

Information we process

  • Account and equipment data you enter when connecting inverters or plants (for example Deye / Huawei identifiers), so the dashboard can show power flow, battery SoC, and forecasts.
  • Contact details (name, phone, and optional message) if you submit a callback, marketplace, BESS order, or consultation form. We use these only to respond to your request.
  • Usage analytics in production via Microsoft Clarity (page views and in-app interaction). This is used to improve the product, not to sell advertising profiles.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the data for tracking across third-party apps and websites for advertising.

Legal bases and retention

We process data to provide the service you request (contract / legitimate interest) and to improve reliability. Contact-form data is kept as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up support. Telemetry tied to your plants is kept while your integration remains active.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you by emailing km220.com@gmail.com.

Children

The app is intended for energy-site operators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest revision. The current version is always at https://220-km.com:9220/privacy.html.
